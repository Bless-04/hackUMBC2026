A GOOD PLACE TO START

Meet your dashboard.

  1. Take a look around.

    Start a demo session to watch a simulated person approach, trigger an alert, and move away.

  2. Bring your camera.

    In Configuration, choose Live camera and its index. Connect your Logitech webcam to the computer running this server.

  3. Add a voice.

    Select your JBL speaker as the system output. Enable voice, Gemini, or Backboard in Configuration; add service keys to .env.

Camera distance is an estimate. This dashboard is a prototype for supervised testing.
